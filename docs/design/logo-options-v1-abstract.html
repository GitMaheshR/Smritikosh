Smritikosh · logo options

Smritikosh means “treasury of memories” (smriti = memory, kosh = store). These six marks are drawn from scratch as simple shapes, in the app’s indigo and teal. Each is shown as the phone app icon, at small sizes, and next to the name on a light and a dark background.

All six are plain geometric shapes (rounded rectangles, circles, arcs and one curve), so none copies a known logo, but nothing here has been checked against trademark registers.
