Smritikosh · memory logo options

Smritikosh means “treasury of memories”. Every option below shows memory directly: a jar of remembered moments, a brain, a photo, an elephant (“never forgets”), an open book, and a head with sparks of thought. All are drawn from scratch in the app’s indigo and teal.

None of these copies a known logo, but they have not been checked against trademark registers. The elephant, brain and head are common symbols, so a quick search is worth doing before you publish the app. Tell me a number (or a mix) and I will use it for the app header, the phone icon, the Windows icon and the tray icon.
